# AcmeCloud FAQ

## What is AcmeCloud?

AcmeCloud is a managed platform for deploying and scaling web applications. It
provides automatic TLS certificates, horizontal scaling, and a built-in content
delivery network.

## How much does it cost?

AcmeCloud has a free tier that includes 512 MB of RAM and 10 GB of bandwidth per
month. Paid plans start at 12 USD per month and scale with usage. Customers can
cancel or change plans at any time without a penalty.

## Which regions are supported?

AcmeCloud currently runs in three regions: us-east, eu-west, and ap-southeast.
A fourth region in South America is planned for next year.

## How do I get support?

Support is available through the in-app chat, by email at support@acmecloud.example,
or through the community forum. Enterprise customers also get a dedicated
support engineer with a response-time guarantee.

## What is the refund policy?

Refunds are issued automatically for any unused portion of a monthly plan when a
customer cancels within the first 14 days. Annual plans can be refunded on a
pro-rated basis after that window.
